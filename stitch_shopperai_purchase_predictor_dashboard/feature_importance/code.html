Feature Deep-Dive: Why PageValues Decides Purchase Intent

Statistical breakdown of the single highest information-gain predictor in the UCI dataset.

calculate Behavioral Mechanism & Formula
Google Analytics Definition:
PageValue = (Transaction Revenue + Total Goal Value) ÷ Unique Pageviews

In practice, PageValues quantifies commercial exposure. A non-zero PageValue proves the visitor interacted with pages that historically concluded in completed transactions (such as checkout cart summaries, active promotional landers, or payment gateways).

tips_and_updates Consequently, browsing duration alone is merely passive engagement, whereas PageValues acts as empirical commitment telemetry.
