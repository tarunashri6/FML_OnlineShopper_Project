Metric Definitions for Viva Discussion

record_voice_over

Plain language statistical justifications for evaluators and commercial stakeholders

Accuracy Cautionary Metric

Overall percentage of correct predictions out of all sessions. Viva Note: In imbalanced data (84.5% non-buyers), a dummy classifier predicting "No" achieves 84.5% accuracy without learning any shopper dynamics.

Precision Marketing Cost Control

When the model predicts "Purchase", how often is it actually correct? Essential for guaranteeing promotional checkout discounts are not wasted on unready casual browsers.

Recall (Sensitivity) Revenue Retention

What percentage of all actual paying buyers did the model successfully identify? Crucial for capturing high-intent cart journeys and mitigating revenue abandonment.

F1-Score (Harmonic Mean) Primary Benchmark

The harmonic mean of Precision and Recall: 2 * (P * R) / (P + R). Penalizes extreme imbalances; selected as our primary objective metric for model ranking.

ROC-AUC Threshold Invariance

Quantifies discriminatory power across all possible cutoffs. Random Forest's 0.924 AUC indicates a 92.4% probability of ranking a randomly chosen buyer higher than a non-buyer.
