Commercial Justification

In real-world e-commerce, offering a 10% coupon to a false positive visitor costs mere cents in margin, whereas missing a high-intent customer completely results in hundreds of dollars in lost conversion value. Hence, maximizing Recall is the primary economic objective.

Viva Examiner Key Summary

Concise academic talking points ready to address evaluators on experimental rigor and sampling integrity.

1
Root Cause Identification

Naturally rare purchase events (15.5%) bias standard loss functions toward predicting the majority class, causing conventional classifiers to favor precision at the cost of catastrophic false-negative rates.

2
Optimal Empirical Fix

SMOTE coupled with hyperparameter-tuned Random Forest elevated purchase recall from 48.6% to 76.5% (+27.9 percentage points), successfully uncovering hidden purchase intent signals.

3
Evaluation Rigor & Defense

Model selection was decided strictly on F1-Score (0.664) and PR-AUC (0.749), formally discarding naive 84.5% accuracy as an invalid diagnostic due to severe class imbalance.
